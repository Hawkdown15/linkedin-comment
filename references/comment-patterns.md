# Comment patterns

Taken from comments that earned replies or reactions on Navodaya posts, plus NT's own record. All examples are original and use invented people.

Every example is a shape, not a template. Replace the details; do not reuse the wording.

## 1. The small detail
**When:** a long story with one striking moment. **Structure:** name the moment, say why it lands, connect to the school. Best default for variant A.

> Delivering parcels through a Pune monsoon while studying for the CA finals, Meera.
>
> That part never makes the convocation photo, and it's the part we'd ask about first.
>
> JNV Satara would be proud of those nights.

## 2. The number
**When:** the post has a figure. **Structure:** state the jump, credit the people, look ahead.

> From 2 finalists to 11 in three years, and all of it from one ground at JNV Kullu.
>
> Results like that only happen when a coach stays long after the bell.
>
> Well done to the students and to the whole sports team. 👏

## 3. Full circle
**When:** returning to campus as a teacher, speaker or donor. **Structure:** then and now, in their own terms, then what the juniors gain.

> Same corridor, different side of the staff room door, Vikram.
>
> Your students at JNV Hassan now have a teacher who knows exactly what that first homesick week feels like.
>
> That is a rare gift to walk in with. 🎓

## 4. The carried habit
**When:** the post credits Navodaya for strength elsewhere. **Structure:** the later challenge, the habit from JNV, the link. Best default for variant B.

> Night shifts in a Leeds hospital at 23, and nobody there knew it had started with a 5:30 am whistle in Bastar.
>
> This is what seven years of hostel life leaves in a person, Asha. You stop waiting for someone to look after things.

## 5. The bond between strangers
**When:** two Navodayans discovering each other. **Structure:** the moment, why no explanation was needed.

> A shared mess menu is apparently all it takes to skip the introductions, Rohan. 😄
>
> Two schools 1,200 km apart, and you still finished each other's stories.
>
> Only Navodayans will fully understand this one.

## 6. The nostalgia nod
**When:** hostel memories. **Structure:** pick their funniest specific, then the affection. Keep any rule-breaking light and leave out names.

> That walk back before the 5:30 attendance deserves its own chapter, Sameer.
>
> Every batch has a story like this, and every batch swears theirs was the closest call.
>
> Which friend still brings it up at reunions? 😄

## 7. The teacher credit
**When:** a tribute, or a success that names a teacher. **Structure:** name the teacher as the post does, name what they did, widen to all teachers at JNVs. Do not invent a teacher.

> Mrs. Thomas staying back to check your essays after evening study says everything, Divya.
>
> So many Navodayan stories begin with one teacher who saw something early.
>
> We hope she gets to read this. ❤️

## 8. The community event
**When:** reunions and alumni meets. **Structure:** something particular from the event, the people who ran it, what it does for the community. Never only "great event".

> Bringing batches from 1994 to 2022 into one hall in Nagpur takes months of unseen phone calls.
>
> Well done to the organising team.
>
> Which batch travelled the furthest this time?

## 9. The foundation's work
**When:** an alumni body supports students. **Structure:** the specific support, who benefits, the cycle of giving back.

> A mock interview week for final-year students, run by alumni who sat in those same chairs.
>
> This is what the Navodaya family looks like when it gets organised.
>
> Thank you to the volunteers who gave their weekends. 🙌

## 10. The school achievement
**When:** a teacher or school celebrates students, including children. **Structure:** the achievement and the team, nothing personal about any child.

> A working water sensor built in the JNV Dharwad tinkering lab, from sketch to prototype in one term.
>
> Well done to the students and to their mentor.
>
> Ideas like this are how a science room turns into a future engineer's first memory.

## 11. The feature invite (variant D only)
**When:** an adult Navodayan with a strong journey. **Structure:** one line of appreciation, one soft line. No link, no "DM us now". Past invite earned 3 reactions.

> Fifteen years from JNV Gadag to leading a rail project in Nairobi, Prakash.
>
> If you're ever open to it, we'd love to hear this story properly on Navodayans Talk.
