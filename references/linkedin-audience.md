## Who comments

- **Working alumni** are the core. Their headlines often end in "Navodayan" or "JNV", or list it beside the employer.
- **Teachers and PE staff at JNVs** post proudly about students.
- **Alumni bodies and foundations** post regularly: NConverge Foundation, Ex Navodayan Foundation, APNAA JNV, UDAAN, DakshanA Alumni Network, Navodayan Travel, Navodayans@JNU.
- **Current students** post too, often under 18 (see the safety rules).

How they identify: "JNV Purnea, 87 batch", "JNV Agra, 1991 to 1998", "Batch of 2021", house names ("Udaigiri House").

Comment types, most to least common: one-word congratulations and emoji; short pride lines; identity declarations; mini-stories; questions (rare).

Language in comments: mostly English; Hindi on Hindi prompts; light Hinglish ("Mujhe bhi banaya").

## What earns likes and replies

- **Threads are nearly empty.** Posts with 20 to 60 reactions often have 0 to 6 comments, mostly "Congratulations". One 100-plus-reaction post had 2. A specific comment is often the only real one there.
- **Almost no comment passes 3 reactions.** The ones that reach 2 or 3 either state an identity ("JNV Purnea, 87 batch") or add a story.
- **Authors reply when the comment is about their detail**, and alumni reply with their own JNV and batch.
- **Brand vs personal:** NT's comments (1 to 3 reactions) sit level with personal ones. Nobody pushed back on a brand comment. Too little data to say more.
