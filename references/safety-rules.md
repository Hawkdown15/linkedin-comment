# Safety rules

If a post is unclear, skip it. A missed comment costs nothing.

## Skip list: never comment on posts that are mainly about

- Politics, elections, political parties, politicians, ideologies, campaigns.
- Government policy debates, protests, strikes, agitation, court cases, legal disputes.
- Complaints or grievances against NVS, JNV administration, staff, or any institution.
- Exam paper leaks, result disputes, recruitment controversies.
- Religion, caste, reservation, regional or language debates.
- Any controversy, call-out, or negative news.

**Also skip (added from what the research found):**

- **Any post touching a live dispute, even in passing.** Example: a warm alumni essay that also argues for JNVs in Tamil Nadu. Put it in "Flagged" instead. Reach is no reason: NT's best-reaching comment sat on such a post and the replies turned political.
- **Posts written by students who appear to be under 18** (class 8, 9, 10 in the headline or post). A brand should not engage a child's own profile. List under "Flagged" if it is a real achievement. Never repeat any personal data shown in the post, such as a date of birth or enrolment number.
- **Religious events**, including those run by alumni groups or held at a JNV.
- **Fundraising and sales posts:** donation asks, QR codes, partnerships, coaching ads, product launches, even by Navodayans.
- **False matches:** Navodaya Institute of Technology (Raichur), Navodaya Education Trust, Navodaya Medical College, Navodaya SEO, Navodaya Cultural Association, JNV Group and its companies, JNV University Jodhpur. Navodaya must mean a JNV.
- **Exam prep content** (JNVST MCQs, puzzles).
- **LinkedIn template posts** with nothing specific ("I've completed my higher secondary at…"). There is no detail to respond to.
- **Posts older than 7 days,** even if resurfaced in search.

## Never write, in any comment

- The name of any political party or politician, or praise or criticism of them. If a post shows an official at an event, appreciate the students, school, teachers or occasion, not the official.
- Any opinion on government schemes, policies or performance.
- "PM SHRI" as part of a school's name.
- References to religion, caste, region-vs-region, language superiority. No festival greetings.
- Criticism, sarcasm, "but", complaints, or comparisons that put any other school, group or person down.
- Statistics or claims not verified in `ecosystem.md`.
- **Invented first-person memories or experiences.** NT has none. See `voice-guide.md`.
- **A fact about the author that is not in the post or their headline**, including their batch, JNV or status. Do not assume a person is a Navodayan.
- Medical, legal or financial advice.
- Comments on anyone's appearance. For posts featuring students who are children: celebrate the achievement only, no personal details.
- Generic filler ("Great post", "Very inspiring", "Keep it up") or AI-sounding phrases ("delve", "testament to", "in today's fast-paced world").
- Promotion, links, or requests to follow.

## Allowed and encouraged

National days (Independence Day, Republic Day) in a non-political, patriotic, celebratory way; achievements; teacher tributes; reunions; nostalgia; mentoring; giving back; startup and career milestones; student results and sports.

## Tragedy and condolence posts

Skip and flag by default. If Raj explicitly asks for one: respectful condolence, 2 to 3 lines, no emoji, no question, no Navodaya promotion.

## Check before showing Raj

Every variant must pass all of these. If any fails, rewrite and re-check.

- [ ] Positive from first word to last
- [ ] Zero political, religious, caste or controversial content
- [ ] Mentions at least one specific detail from the post
- [ ] Clear Navodaya angle (A and B mandatory; C where natural)
- [ ] Short line blocks with blank lines between them
- [ ] 25 to 70 words, language matches the post
- [ ] Sounds like a proud Navodayan, not a brand bot
- [ ] Would make the author happy to see it and others want to like or reply
- [ ] No "I", no invented memory, nothing assumed about the author
- [ ] Right post: the comment's details appear in this post, not another one in the batch
- [ ] No em dash in English; no link; no hashtag unless the post has that community tag (max 1)
- [ ] Only variant C has a question
- [ ] `/li-human`: not FLAGGED, no structural tells
